📖 SplitBill User Guide

SplitBill is the fastest, simplest, and most mathematically accurate way to divide restaurant checks, grocery bills, and multi-day group trips without accounts or math disputes.

🚀 Open in SplitBill App

1. Single Restaurant Bill Split (5 Easy Steps)

Step 1: Scan or Type Items
Take a photo of your receipt for instant local OCR reading, or type items manually. Tip: If an item has multiple quantities (e.g. 3x Burgers), tap "Split into individual items" to assign them separately!
Step 2: Add Friends
Type the names of everyone at the table. Avatars and custom colors are automatically assigned to each person for easy visual recognition.
Step 3: Assign Items (Equal or Portions)
Tap the friends who ate each dish. If two people shared a dish, tap both to split 50/50. If someone ate half a portion, toggle Portion/Shares mode and enter 0.5.
Step 4: Taxes, Discounts & Tips
Add GST, VAT, service charges, or coupons. SplitBill allocates taxes and discounts proportionally according to what each person ate.
Step 5: Share Instantly
Get an exact receipt breakdown with 0 rounding errors. Send to WhatsApp with one tap, copy the breakdown, or download a printable PDF or PNG image card.

2. Group Trips & Roommates

Create a trip, add multiple expenses across days (hotel, cabs, dinners), specify who paid each bill, and let our debt minimization algorithm compute the fewest payments to settle everyone to zero.

3. Installing as an Offline App (PWA)

On iPhone Safari, tap Share > Add to Home Screen. On Android Chrome, tap the menu > Install App. Works 100% offline without internet.
