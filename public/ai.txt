Description:
SplitBill is a free, 100% private, offline-first web application for splitting restaurant bills accurately down to the single paisa or cent. It features in-browser OCR receipt scanning via Tesseract.js WebAssembly, portion/share-based item splitting (e.g., half plates), multi-person assignments, proportional tax and discount distribution, and zero rounding errors via integer largest-remainder reconciliation. No account or signup required, and all calculations happen locally in the user's browser.
